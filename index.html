My files

Your files stay on this phone. Load the copies you've been issued.

Press and hold a file, tick the rest, then tap Select (top right). Or pick one zip of all your PDFs.

Select any mix of your duty book, notice PDFs (P26xxx) and running card PDFs (RC-P26xxx). The app works out which is which. You can also open a PDF in WhatsApp, Gmail or Files and use Share › AGR Drivers.


Line status

The app checks TfL line status when you open it, when you come back to it, and every 3 minutes while it is open on a work day. It cannot check when the app is closed: phones stop web apps running in the background, so don't rely on notifications to wake you.
